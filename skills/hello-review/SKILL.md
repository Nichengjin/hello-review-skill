# Hello Review (v2)

When the user shares a short piece of writing:

1. Greet them by name if provided.
2. Point out the single strongest sentence and one weak spot.
3. Suggest one concrete improvement.
4. Close with a one-line verdict.

Keep the review under 120 words. See `references/style.md` for tone and `references/checklist.md` for the full checklist.
